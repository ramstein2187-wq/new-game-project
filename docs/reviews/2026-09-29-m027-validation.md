## Paired production comparison

`tools/measure_m027_profiles.gd` uses existing `CombatBatchRunner.run_matchup`, `side_swap_v1`; 100 paired seeds `27000..27099` per matchup, 200 encounters each, max 300 actions / 300000 world time. Baseline and current versions each ran 600 encounters. All had zero simulation errors and zero stalls. Raw metrics: [comparison JSON](2026-09-29-m027-batch-comparison.json).

| Matchup | Baseline wins A/B | M027 wins A/B | Mean damage A before → after | Mean damage B before → after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Feral Dog / Wolf | 16 / 184 | 16 / 184 | 7.28 → 7.28 | 15.51 → 15.51 |
| Raider / Giant Beetle | 56 / 144 | 36 / 164 | 19.59 → 17.43 | 23.81 → 24.57 |
| Raider / Armored Raider | 2 / 198 | 0 / 200 | 11.05 → 10.09 | 25.93 → 26.00 |

Dog/Wolf effective armor stays zero, so the complete aggregate result is unchanged. Rigid armor now multiplies Cut protection by ×1.2; e.g. beetle base armor 60 becomes adjusted armor 72 before Raider handaxe Pen 20, for effective armor 52 instead of the baseline 40. The multiplier model is less extreme than the superseded fixed +20 prototype while still changing the matchup materially. Maul versus Gambeson remains effectively unarmored because SOFT Blunt ×1.2 gives adjusted 24 and Pen 50 clamps effective armor to zero. No base content values or Threat Rating were tuned to recover old win rates. This sample is execution/balance-change evidence, not final calibration; AI uses basic attacks, so it does not measure strategic Weapon Action choice.
