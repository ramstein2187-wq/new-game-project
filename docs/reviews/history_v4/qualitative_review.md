## Findings and corrections

The first corpus exposed short redesign/signal-fixation dates. Final output now spans at least 30 years after activation for population redesign, and at least 24 for signal fixation. Failures/departures/continuing automation carry explicit observations instead of substituting a generic collapse word.

The first Claim pass used faction parity and sometimes overwrote a specialized Continuity claim with a generic project sentence. Final Claims consume actual identity/adaptive stance, preserve specialized Scar interpretations and expose opposing bodily/memory continuity interpretations (seed9). Documented Ark interdiction is a physical cause, while intent remains unknown. Targeted-extermination Claims acknowledge signed policy and registered targets. Actual target creation and facility construction are validated, including malicious substitutions with unrelated existing entities.

## Final judgment

No selected raw history resolves Last Descent's killer/discovery, anomalous disappearance, Far-Sky origin, soul transfer, same-person continuity or a unified present Preservator will. Regional knowledge and developer subsystem provenance remain different. Two-Project/two-Scar histories can be dense (seed9/362); the budget is a concrete upper bound, not proof that every future writer will find pacing ideal. Facilities are projected last-recorded conditions, not a continuously simulated state. Culture is a derived preference and can contain internal tension; it is not one metaphysical truth assigned to a faction.
