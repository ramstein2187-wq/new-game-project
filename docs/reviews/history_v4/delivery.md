## Final ten review answers

1. **History itself changes under SF conditions.** Resource mobilization, specialist institutions, generation-scale redesign, population/custody records, incompatible identity registries and current ruins affect later evidence; this is more than extra narrative labels. Ordinary political DAGs remain intact.
2. **Bizarre events are not universally attributed to Preservator.** Human, natural, Observer-legacy and unknown pressures coexist. Biological loss, consciousness anomalies and Last Descent retain unknown causes.48.12% worlds record some Preservator activity, not universal omnipotence.
3. **Unknown stays unknown.** Objective data, author constraints, mutation tests and Claims preserve reserved fields. Signal/echo/mismatch/correlation does not become an alien or hidden-system answer.
4. **No covert philosophical resolution.** Consciousness survival, same person, soul transfer and machine personhood remain unsettled; bodily and memory continuity are competing cultural beliefs.
5. **New shipping rare content is reachable.** All Project/Scar/Response/Discovery types exceed1% exposure in5,000 worlds. The close1.12% fracture threshold and intentionally rarer inherited orbital motifs are disclosed; three gated entries are not mislabeled active.
6. **Spectacle is bounded.** At most two Projects and two major Scars;25.64% worlds have neither. Dense samples exist and pacing remains a future human author judgment, not something automated validation can settle.
7. **Projects leave provenance.** Stages carry actual actors, population registers, institution/labor data, facility construction/status and later custody; identity/culture consume those source IDs. Gameplay simulation is deferred.
8. **Projects and Scars are separate.** Success/partial/abandonment without Scars, catastrophe-created Scars and standalone Scars all occur in the corpus.
9. **Population rights remain enforced.** No invented lineage/species/Origin; clones stay cohorts/history, modification stays human variation, machine assimilation remains a recorded process.
10. **Different interpretations remain possible.** Seed19 opposes Closed Sky/Skyward Hunger; seed9 opposes bodily/memory continuity; seed5 faith/skeptical interpretations share one Cognitive Echo.
