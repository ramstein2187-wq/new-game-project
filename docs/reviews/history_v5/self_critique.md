# What I got wrong

Revision1 passed deterministic/schema tests and reached100 Traces,40 Questions
and60 interaction variants across5000 worlds. That demonstrated enumeration,
not semantic validity. I treated a theme as sufficient provenance, cycled
material labels through phases, and accepted an interaction family without
checking the variant's object. These mistakes produced future facilities in
earlier phases, clone laws before clone birth, and machine compacts from ordinary
human maintenance. Natural change incorrectly inherited a biotechnology
capability from a shared terminal Scar definition.

The fully read20 independent worlds scored8.975/7.135/8.300/8.495/8.265/5.695/
8.555/6.440 in the requested metric order. Non-repetition and gameplay potential
are substantially below their goals. All20 had generic contexts, mismatched
hooks, duplicated information, repetitive Claims and insufficient current
stakes. At least18 had premature material staging. Seeds1896,1910,1815 ranked
best;4772,2239,4255 ranked worst by the declared unweighted eight-score mean.
The per-world notes preserve all40 concrete hooks and defect evidence.

I also mislabeled a projection-preservation fraction as a causal ratio. An
objective effect remaining visible does not mean the event is explained. The
final report must separate projection coverage, actual causal edges,
prerequisites, associations, and strong internal episode chains.

   and factual social-record gates. Preserve the existing v2/v3/v4 fixtures.
   Add bounded discovery traces without identifying unknown origins.
2. Preserve actual facility/cohort/civic subjects and source-effect paths.
   Partition Trace assertions by material role; include exact primitive and
   social effects, not a whole-event copy on every object.
3. Require a semantic anchor for each Question, scope it to its actual subject,
   include that anchor in selected evidence, and reserve selection priority for
   substantive Projects/Scars/discoveries before ordinary civic questions.
   Use plural/comparison wording where the scope spans communities.
4. Match each interaction variant to material affordances. Runtime tags express
   unverified present conditions and must be checked by consumers; they cannot
   manufacture historical machine behavior, hostile occupants or biological
   procedures.
5. Give consequences concrete evidence/subject/current stakeholders, a current
   decision and execution requirements. A recorded past procedure is not an
   automatically usable present treatment. Preserve unknowns and moral choice.
6. Remove repeated cause IDs in v5 and introduce renderer-only factual clauses
   keyed by formation, phase, relation and custody. No narrative RNG enters the
   canonical generator.
7. Add negative semantic checks, run the full completion gate, then a new5000
   corpus. Only afterward draw a disjoint OS-random20, read every full raw file,
   and report honest before/after results and remaining soft-target misses.
