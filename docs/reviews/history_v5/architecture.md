# Sparse History and playable archaeology architecture

Generation5 / architecture3 / history-v5-authored-2 / history-v5-archaeology-2. Independent generation entry,
operation-first topology and episode composer replace the full regional collapse
rhythm. Reuse authoritative entity/population/effect grammar and bounded Project/
Scar prerequisites; historical2/3/4 stay explicit and frozen. No save upgrader.

Layers:
1. Canon locks/reservations and approved human populations.
2. Objective facts with explicit causes only where recorded. Entity lifecycle and
   political-parent provenance do not explain present lifestyle or motivation.
3. Replayed present from objective effects; current records are last observations.
4. Derived historical traces and source-specific evidence assertions, never new truth.
5. Historical questions with recoverable/contested/open classification and3–5
   independent traces in distinct access contexts where available.
6. Structured investigation hooks, evidence accumulation and permission proposals.
   Permissions enable current choices; no implicit world mutation or ethical answer.
7. Culture/Claims, independent normative interpretation; rendering never changes facts.

Historical association is stored separately from cause_event_ids. Coexistence is
the default; no closure requirement spans unrelated episodes. Region epochs leave
gaps. Strong Project/Scar internal prerequisites remain. Ordinary political,
social and site histories supply archaeology even when no spectacular Project occurs.

Risks: preserving exact compatibility while adding routing; evidence granularity
and manufactured redundancy; real family differences; prose/hook repetition;
large artifact sizes. Hard safety tests and5000 world distributions precede two
independent random20 raw reviews with a mandatory general revision between rounds.
Soft-score misses are reported honestly. Player knowledge, map placement and executing future
world actions remain integration boundaries; consumer APIs are implemented here.

## Revision2 provenance contracts

`HistoryV5Materials` maps an ordinary narrative or exact social incident ID to
its authored observation list. It never maps an entire social family to every
stage. Manifests retain a registered participant as `reference_id` and the actual
recorded site/cohort/settlement as `recorded_subject_id`; a past home may be
retired while its history remains observable. Project/Scar manifests use their
authored facility and stage-specific `material_theme`. Natural/unclassified
morphological change supplies no biotechnology capability.

A Trace has a stable `trace/event/archetype` ID, category, actual subject,
source entity/event IDs, access contexts, custodian, exact assertion paths
(`source_effect_index`, `source_record_id`, field/value), anchor records and
current-operation=`unverified`. Documentary, physical, institutional and living
categories project different record/effect roles. Partial overlap is legitimate
corroboration; a theme label never supplies a missing physical fact. Discovery
objects retain origin/purpose/personhood unknown and may remain minor discoveries
when no independent corroborating historical source exists.
Nested assertion values are deep copies: annotating a dossier cannot modify the
original population or measurement dictionaries/arrays. The regression directly
mutates those derived values and verifies the objective timeline remains exact.

A Question has a definition-specified actual anchor, scope and classification.
Actual-subject questions partition by facility/cohort/site. Civic/migration/trade
comparisons explicitly use a regional plural scope. Custody can supplement the
same subject and creates a separate current-contact context; it cannot mix
unrelated sites. Selection first prefers diverse substantive Scar/Project/social
subjects, then ordinary questions. Selected evidence includes the actual premise,
3–5 distinct Traces, at least two events and two contexts. The investigation
session must learn an anchor as well as reach the corroboration thresholds.

Each interaction variant declares eligible categories/archetypes and required
runtime tags. Guidance inspection targets departure/reentry records, machine
handshakes target actual machine evidence, and biological protocols require
authorized access/consent and a current approved protocol. Hostility, live
interfaces, water intrusion and environmental control are conditional consumer
checks, never generated assertions that such conditions currently exist.

Consequences include the actual subject, evidence/anchor IDs, current recorded
stakeholders, a present decision and execution requirements. Candidate issue
positions derive from current roles and require dialogue confirmation; existing
relationship scores retain their objective source IDs. No chain explaining the
historical development of those current positions is added. Proposals cannot
execute treatment, reveal a reserved Canon answer or overwrite historical truth.

`HistoryInvestigation.perform()` collects one referenced Trace after context/tag
checks. `permissions()` returns only corroborated proposals. `propose()` returns
the proposal and its outstanding execution requirements; it does not perform
combat, theft, activation, dialogue or destruction. Map/Actor/contact binding and
action execution belong to subsequent consumers, with access checks at execution.

## Rendering and compatibility

Renderer event/Trace choices use `history-renderer/5`.34 Project phase keys each
have two complete factual alternatives; formation/custody/relation keys have
three. Same-event identical Claims are grouped with every claimant/confidence
retained. Reordering alternatives preserves renderer choice; adding an alternative
may change prose while leaving canonical history, topology, chronology, population,
Project/Scar outcomes and Culture untouched. Raw outputs include all objective
effects, historical/current factions, remains, all Traces, selected Questions,
their hooks/consequences and every Claim through grouping.

Frozen128 seeds/version are recorded in `tests/fixtures/history_v5_legacy.json`.
The fixture writer refuses an existing file. Shared changes consist of explicit
version5 routing, a sparse-lifecycle opt-in and polymorphic RNG dispatch retaining
the original v4 default. Historical tests/tools request4 explicitly. Existing
Culture/identity queries use the v4 read-only compatibility view for4/5; they do
not serialize interpretation as historical facts.

## Metric definitions and limits

The event explanation proxy counts events having a recorded cause/prerequisite;
it is not a claim that current lifestyle or motives are explained. Internal edge
counts require both endpoints in the same Project/Scar scope. Topology edges,
known availability prerequisites across scopes and remaining cross-episode edges
are separately counted. Associations count in none of them. Projection coverage
measures retained effects and is explicitly not a causal-quality score.

Exposure measures worlds containing an archetype, not row definitions or repeated
objects. Hook/action figures are consumer-contract reachability. Documentary-only
and passive-reading-only contract fractions are distinct from engine integration:
zero current quests/encounters/actions are bound or executed by this work.
