## Implementation decisions

- `AttackAction.BASE_COST = 1000` is the single normal-melee time owner; `ActorDefinition.attack_cost` was removed.
- Attack data moved out of `CombatSpecies`; Actor instances copy the active weapon or natural `AttackDefinition`.
- `BodyInstance` selects the best functional capability parts, supports required counts, and preserves the existing -2 injury penalty without inventing a new formula.
- Armor remains one value per body part. Natural armor covers the full body; prototype equipment covers body-part types and does not stack in supplied loadouts.
- Combat RNG order is D20 -> hit location -> damage dice -> optional armor -> application.
- Historical completed milestone documents retain their historical fixed-damage and 1250/1000 timing statements.
