## Implementation

- `history_v4.json`: authored projects, stage observations, scars, discoveries,
  pressure/response/collapse compatibility and capability/content gates.
- `HistoryV4Planner`: bounded 0–2 long-duration projects; at most two major Scars;
  compatible regional and additional response chains; separately seeded discovery.
- `history_record`: objective participant/reference/project data, replayed by
  `HistoryProjector` into Project, Scar, facility and historical-observation ledgers.
- `HistoryV4Compatibility`: canonical migration and an explicit decoded view for
  existing political/population/social validation and identity evidence. It never
  treats new effects as old effects and never changes a historical fixture.
- `HistoryV4Validator`: chronology, causal prerequisites, actual references,
  authored data, mysteries, content permissions, phase chains, bounds, projection,
  Claim provenance and canonical vocabulary.
- Existing culture content gains canonical Faith/Religious/Sacred IDs and factual
  Project/Scar hooks; no large Doctrine expansion or philosophical answer.

Project outcomes include success, partial success, abandonment, unknown outcome
and catastrophe. Scars may arise without a Project. Construction, labor quotas,
specialist institutions, population records, damaged/active facilities and later
custody carry actual event references. Current archival custody does not fabricate
biological ancestry. Project and Scar remain different data categories.
